# TMS (Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation)

The Hoplab runs TMS studies in room PSI 00.57, with a DuoMAG stimulator and Brainsight neuronavigation.

!!! danger "Safety first"
    Never stimulate anyone (participants or colleagues) without a completed safety screening. Read the [safety and emergency procedures](tms-safety.md) before your first session.
