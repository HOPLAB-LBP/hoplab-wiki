FriGO is KU Leuven's cold storage service for long-term archiving of research datasets that are not published in a data repository (e.g. RDR) but are still relevant and need to be kept for reproducibility or reuse.

 **Hoplab/NeuroSPACE does not currently have a FriGO project**; for now, SharePoint serves as our time capsule for long-term data storage, but setting one up may be considered in the future, depending on our archiving needs.

For more information about FriGO and how to request a project, see the [KU Leuven RDM documentation on FriGO](https://rdm-docs.icts.kuleuven.be/frigo/index.html).
